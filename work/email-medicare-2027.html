Will Your Medicare Coverage Still Work for You in 2027?

Medicare Annual Enrollment is here.

October 15 – December 7

REVIEW YOUR MEDICARE OPTIONS

The coverage that worked well for you this year may not necessarily be the best fit for 2027.

Plans can change. Your prescriptions, doctors, health needs and budget can change, too. That makes Medicare’s Annual Enrollment Period an important opportunity to take another look at your coverage.

Before You Keep Your Current Coverage, Ask:
✓ Are my doctors still in-network?
✓ Are my prescriptions still covered the way I expect?
✓ Have my costs or benefits changed?
✓ Are there other Medicare options I should consider?

You don’t have to sort through all of that on your own.
